Most people understand that exercise is important for good health.

But what kind of exercise truly helps people stay healthy and live longer?
Interestingly, epidemiological research suggests that tennis is one of the sports most strongly associated with longevity.

Research Shows Tennis May Extend Life Expectancy

A large long-term study conducted in Denmark followed approximately 80,000 people over a 25-year period. The results suggested that different types of exercise may influence life expectancy in different ways.

Among the activities studied, tennis showed one of the strongest associations with longevity, with an estimated increase in life expectancy of nearly 10 years compared with people who did not exercise.

Type of Exercise Increase in Life Expectancy
No Exercise Baseline
Tennis +9.7 years
Badminton +6.2 years
Soccer +4.7 years
Cycling +3.7 years
Jogging +3.2 years
Swimming +3.4 years

* Based on findings reported by Schnohr P. et al., Mayo Clinic Proceedings (2018)

Why Is Tennis So Good for Health?

Tennis naturally combines several important elements of healthy physical activity.

Running, hitting, stopping, and changing direction all help engage the body in a variety of ways. In addition, players must think about their opponent’s movements and strategies, meaning that tennis stimulates not only the body, but also the brain.

Social Connection Is Also Important for Health

Tennis is a social sport that naturally encourages conversation and interaction. Maintaining social connections is considered an important factor in supporting long-term health and quality of life.

Keep Playing Tennis for Better Health

Tennis is not only a competitive sport, but also an excellent form of exercise for maintaining health.
It can be enjoyed at many ages and skill levels, making it a lifelong activity for many people.

The key is to enjoy the game and continue at a comfortable pace without overdoing it.

Lesson-Based Tennis Can Provide Even More Health Benefits

For people playing tennis mainly for health and fitness, lesson-based tennis programs offer several advantages.

  • Sessions are usually designed with safe and balanced exercise from warm-up to match play
  • Listening to instruction and learning new skills can stimulate the brain
  • Conversation and interaction help maintain social connections
  • Warm-up exercises and form checks can improve safety
  • Visible improvement makes it easier to stay motivated and continue playing

For these reasons, lesson-based tennis programs can be considered an excellent lifelong exercise habit that supports healthy aging and longevity.
